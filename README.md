# 🌾 Gajapati Krushi Connect

> Open-Source Digital Agriculture Portal & Tribal D2C Marketplace for Gajapati District, Odisha.

---

## 📌 About the Project

**Gajapati Krushi Connect** is a community-driven web platform designed to support farmers, Women Self-Help Groups (WSHGs), and agricultural extension officers across all 7 blocks of Gajapati District (Paralakhemundi, Mohana, R. Udayagiri, Rayagada, Gumma, Nuagada, and Kashinagar).

The portal bridges local agricultural knowledge, organic farming practices, and direct market access for signature regional produce like **Chandragiri Arabica Coffee, Organic Turmeric, Mandia (Millet Mission), and Mohana Pineapple**.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 📖 **Gajapati Specialty Crop Wiki:** Sowing calendars, soil requirements, and organic packages of practices.
- 🛒 **Tribal & WSHG D2C Marketplace:** Direct farm-to-consumer listings with zero middleman markup.
- 🗺️ **Interactive Block Map:** Interactive visual canvas mapping major crops and extension contact points by block.
- 🌤️ **Localized Weather & Agromet Advisories:** High-elevation rainfall alerts and Mahendragiri runoff warnings.
- 🧮 **Agronomy Tools:** Organic FYM/fertilizer estimator and plant diagnostic helper.
- 📜 **PRA & Extension Guides:** Participatory Rural Appraisal frameworks and Odisha Millet Mission integration steps.
