R. Udayagiri & Chandragiri

Chandragiri Arabica Coffee

Coffee arabica L.

Grown in high-altitude shade plantations in Chandragiri and R. Udayagiri hills intercropped with silver oak and pepper.

Optimal Sowing/Planting: June – August (Monsoon)
Soil Requirement: Deep, well-drained acidic soil (pH 6.0-6.5)
Harvest Period: November – February
Nuagada & Mohana

Gajapati Organic Turmeric

Curcuma longa

Renowned for high curcumin content and rich natural aroma. Grown organically without synthetic fertilizers by tribal farmers.

Sowing Window: May – June (Pre-Monsoon)
Soil Requirement: Friable red loamy soil
Harvest Period: January – March (9 Months)
Gumma & Rayagada

Mandia / Finger Millet (Ragi)

Eleusine coracana

Key crop under the Odisha Millet Mission. Highly climate-resilient crop requiring minimal water and resistant to pests.

Sowing Window: July – August
Soil Requirement: Red lateritic & light gravelly soil
Harvest Period: October – November
Mohana & R. Udayagiri

Mohana Queen Pineapple

Ananas comosus

Juicy, sweet pineapple variety cultivated across sloped hill tracts of Mohana block with direct market linkage to Berhampur and Bhubaneswar.

Suckers Planting: July – September
Soil Requirement: Sandy loam with high organic matter
Peak Harvest: May – July
Rayagada & Gosani

Gajapati Raw Cashew

Anacardium occidentale

Extensively grown on hill slopes to prevent soil erosion while providing substantial seasonal income to tribal households.

Planting Window: June – August
Soil Requirement: Red laterite soils
Harvest Period: March – May
Paralakhemundi & Kashinagar

Vansadhara Alluvial Paddy

Oryza sativa

High-yielding paddy cultivation supported by canal irrigation from Vansadhara river in the southern plains of Gajapati.

Kharif Sowing: June – July
Soil Requirement: Clay loam & alluvial deposit
Harvest Period: November – December

PRA & Tribal Extension Frameworks

Participatory Rural Appraisal (PRA) tools and government scheme integration guidelines tailored for Gajapati.

PRA Methodology

Transect Walk in Hilly Tribal Hamlets

A systematic walk with tribal elders from hilltop Podu fields to valley bottom paddy lands to map soil erosion zones, natural streams, and traditional crop diversity.

  • Identify hill slope runoff points in Mohana & Gumma.
  • Map natural springs for community micro-irrigation.
  • Document local indigenous seed varieties.
Odisha Govt Scheme

Odisha Millet Mission (OMM) Integration

Guidelines for WSHGs and FPOs in Gajapati to register under the Odisha Millet Mission for direct procurement incentives and processing unit support.

  • Incentives up to ₹12,000 / hectare over 3 years.
  • Support for setting up local Mandia processing thresher units.
